Gestor Aramis

A private workspace for the Aramis team to plan content, prepare production material and review finished work.

Marketing and design coordinate client posts, reels and stories in one place, with scripts, planned dates, files and a record of revisions.

Open workspaceRead the privacy policy

How the team uses it

  1. Plan

    Organize monthly client content, write scripts and captions, and set planned publication dates.

  2. Produce

    Hand ready pieces to design with their instructions and source material. Browse previews and download the files needed for editing.

  3. Review

    Upload finished work, review an exact delivery version and either approve it or return it to production with comments.

Access to the workspace

Aramis provides accounts to authorized team members. A team account is required to view client work and materials.

Staff sign-in is separate from the Google account connected to Drive. The team uses a shared Drive connection managed by the workspace administrator.

The application is currently used internally. Social media publication is handled outside the workspace.

Why Gestor Aramis connects to Google Drive

Drive stores the team's source footage, images and finished deliveries. The integration creates the workspace's folders, uploads files, reads material added directly to linked folders, shows previews, downloads originals and preserves delivery revisions.

Google's requested permission allows access to all Drive files available to the connected account. The application's server limits its file operations to the folders and materials linked to this workspace. This broader permission is needed to read and move to the trash material added directly through Drive, as well as files uploaded through the application.

Moving source material to the trash requires an explicit team action. Connecting Drive does not make files public or change their sharing permissions. See the Google Drive section of our privacy policy for details.
